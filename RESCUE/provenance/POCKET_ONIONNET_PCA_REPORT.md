> dropping Pearson R from 0.504 → 0.3582 (as shown in ONIONNET_TRANSFER_REPORT.md).

> Random Forest (ADD-I). This preserves the pocket signal while adding structural
> contact information in a controlled, low-dimensional form.

## Why This Works (or Why It Doesn't)
1. **Pocket-restriction**: Limiting to ~21 pocket/neighbor residues removes
   the 78+ residue noise that differs between HIV-1 and HIV-2 but doesn't
   affect binding. This is why BASE-A achieves R=0.578 vs. global R=0.504.
2. **PCA compression**: The 10,309-D ONION-Net feature is highly redundant.
   PCA extracts the top binding-geometry-correlated components without
   adding the 10k-D curse of dimensionality on top of the 21D pocket signal.
3. **Target weighting**: Upweighting high-resistance variants forces the model
   to learn signals in the rare high-affinity region (ADD-I improvement).
4. **Naive concatenation problem**: Stacking 10,309D on top of 21D pocket
   features gives the RF 99.8% ONION-Net dimensions to split on, effectively
   ignoring the pocket signal — exactly what was observed in ONIONNET_TRANSFER_REPORT.
